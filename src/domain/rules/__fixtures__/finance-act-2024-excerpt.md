                                     FINANCE ACT 2024


An Act to provide for the imposition, repeal, remission, alteration and regulation of
taxation, of stamp duties and of duties relating to excise and otherwise to make further
provision in connection with finance; to make provision for supports to employers and
certain businesses and for that purpose to amend the Taxes Consolidation Act 1997, the
Social Welfare Consolidation Act 2005, the Value-Added Tax Consolidation Act 2010
and Part 7 of the Emergency Measures in the Public Interest (Covid-19) Act 2020; and
to provide for related matters.                                    [12th November, 2024]
Be it enacted by the Oireachtas as follows:
