# TCA 1997 s.613

Not chargeable gains: instalment-saving bonuses; prize-bond prizes; compensation or damages for personal or professional injury; betting/lottery winnings; certain superannuation and life-annuity rights; unsecured covenant annual payments; and most disposals of an interest under a settlement by the person for whose benefit it was created.
