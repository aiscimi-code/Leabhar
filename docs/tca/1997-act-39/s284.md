# TCA 1997 s.284 — wear and tear

Where a person carrying on a trade has incurred capital expenditure on machinery or plant for the trade, a wear and tear allowance is made for the chargeable period if the asset belongs to the person, is in use for the trade at the end of the period, and while used for the trade is wholly and exclusively so used.

As enacted, the allowance was 15% of actual cost (renewals included) or 20% of written-down value for road vehicles, scaled for short periods, and capped so that total allowances plus any s.283 initial allowance cannot exceed cost. No s.284 allowance for industrial buildings (s.268). The section also applies, with modifications, to furnished lettings charged under Part 4 Chapter 8.
