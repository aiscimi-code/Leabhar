# TCA 1997 s.299 — allowances to lessees

Where plant is let to the trader on terms that the trader must maintain it and deliver it in good condition, and the burden of wear and tear falls on the trader, then for ss.283 and 284 the trader is treated as having incurred the capital expenditure and as owning the plant.
