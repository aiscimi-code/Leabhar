# Tax and Duty Manual Part 38-01-03b — Guidelines for VAT Registration
# Pages 19–36

## 6. Registering a VAT Application

### 6.1 Section 110 estimates
Section 110 enables Revenue to enforce payment of tax against a taxable person who has failed to submit a VAT return for any taxable period within the prescribed time after the end of that period.

There are different rates of VAT currently applicable to goods and services. The VAT rates database lists products and services. Rating legislation is in the VAT Consolidation Act 2010.

### 6.5 Mandatory eFiling of returns
Mandatory electronic filing of payments and returns using ROS is part of Revenue’s strategy to establish electronic channels as the normal way of conducting tax business. eFiling is mandatory unless an exemption has been granted. Upon registration a VAT2 Advice of Registration letter issues, tailored by trading-tier and accounting basis:
- domestic invoice basis (Appendix 8)
- domestic monies received basis (Appendix 9)
- intra-EU invoice basis (Appendix 10)
- intra-EU monies received basis (Appendix 11).

Irish-language cases receive Irish versions of those letters.

## 7. Disallowing a VAT Application
Applications may be disallowed where there is no accountable activity or the application was made in error. Main reasons:
- Exempt activity (Schedule 1 VATCA 2010) — Appendix 12
- Not accountable under s.5(1)(a) — Appendix 13
- No VATable activity / s.5(1)(a) conditions not met — Appendix 14
- Liquidator/Receiver/Mortgagee joint-option conditions not met — Appendix 15
- No response

### 7.1 Right of appeal
Refusal of registration may be appealed under VATCA s.119(2). Applicants must be told. Template: Appendix 17.

## 8. Postponed Accounting
Available from 11pm on 31 December 2020 to suitably registered accountable persons who acquire goods from outside the EU VAT area. Granted only with a live C&E registration. Register for C&E before VAT / postponed accounting so both can be considered together.

### 8.1 Paper applications
Applicants who cannot register online complete the postponed-accounting section on the paper form and attach the supporting documents in 8.3.

#### 8.1.1 No Irish tax registration number
Complete VAT and C&E first. Indicate postponed accounting on the VAT form and attach 8.3 documents. After both numbers issue, notify Revenue via MyEnquiries (“Tax Registration/Cancellation” → “VAT Postponed Accounting”) or:
- HWFSDiv@revenue.ie
- largecasesdiv@revenue.ie (LCD)
- businesstaxesregistrations@revenue.ie (all others)

### 8.2 Online applications
A Postponed Accounting heading exists in the VAT registration screens. Applicant must answer Yes to importing goods from outside the EU, then whether they want postponed accounting. Criteria: Conditions of VAT — Postponed Accounting. Attach 8.3 documents.

#### 8.2.1 Already VAT-registered, applying later
Register for C&E on the VAT number, then apply in ROS: Other Services → Manage Tax Registrations → Apply for Postponed Accounting of VAT. Sign and submit. ROS issues an acknowledgement with a Notice Number.

Applicants without eRegistration use myEnquiries as above.

#### 8.2.2 Agents
Agents use the same steps, including TAIN link attachment and updated Customer Registration for resident companies and companies in liquidation.

### 8.3 Supporting documentation
- Evidence of current (and previous) business address
- Type, volume and value of goods and services supplied
- Supplier/customer documents (invoices, terms, negotiations, commercial rationale)
- Confirmation of a record-keeping system that is complete, accurate and readily available

Revenue may request further proofs. Failure to supply them in time leads to a Notice of Exclusion. Sample request letter: Appendix 18.

## 9. VAT Number Verification
Irish domestic VAT numbers can be checked from ROS Other Services / MyServices / TAIN services, or MyAccount Manage My Records. Returns valid/invalid. Daily limit of checks applies (tool text cites 20, error text cites 10). Repeat checks of the same number the same day do not count toward the limit.

## 10. EU VAT SME scheme
Introduced 1 January 2025. Where turnover is below the Union threshold (€100,000) and the domestic threshold(s) of the Member State(s) of supply, the person may apply. A person can in limited cases hold both VAT registration and SME-scheme status; if Union turnover exceeds €100,000 the SME registration should cease.
