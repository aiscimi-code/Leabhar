# S.I. 734/2020 — inserts Regulation 14A (postponed accounting)

Come into operation 31 December 2020 at 11:00 p.m.

2. S.I. 639/2010 is amended by inserting after Regulation 14:

**14A.** (1) ‘the Acts’ means the VATCA, the Tax Acts, the Capital Gains Tax Acts, the excise statutes, the Customs Act 2015, and instruments under those enactments.

(2) Subject to paragraph (5), an accountable person may account for tax chargeable under section 3(b) on goods imported into the State by the means in section 53A(1) where the person:

(a) is in compliance with the Acts;
(b) keeps full and true records under section 84;
(c) has not been convicted of an offence under the Acts;
(d) has provided information or documentation specified in paragraph (3) when requested.

(3) Requestable information includes: current and previous business address; solvency and financing; type, volume and value of supplies; supplier/customer terms, negotiations and commercial rationale; evidence of a complete, accurate, readily available record system; latest audited accounts; a declaration that information is correct; a tax clearance certificate under TCA s.1095; any other information Revenue requires for protection of the revenue.

(4) Business address includes a premises address and an address at which manufacturing, retail, storage, administrative or other operations are carried out.

(5) Paragraph (2) does not apply where the person, when requested, fails to demonstrate capacity and capability to account for and pay tax under section 53A(1) on an on-going basis.
