# S.I. 639/2010 reg.25 — moneys-received basis

Sets the terms for VATCA s.80 cash accounting: when tax is treated as due by reference to moneys received rather than invoice date, the records required, and the events that end the authorisation.

Official print (search “25. (1) In this Regulation”): https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/eli/2010/si/639/made/en/print
