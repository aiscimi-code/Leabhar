# S.I. 69/2025 — SME scheme and related VATCA amendments

In force 6 March 2025. Official print: https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/eli/2025/si/69/made/en/print

| Reg | Effect |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Citation; “Act of 2010” = VATCA |
| 3 | VATCA s.2: insert “annual turnover”; extend s.2(4A) to Sch.9 Part 3 |
| 4 | Delete VATCA s.5(2) |
| 5 | Rewrite s.6(1) thresholds around **annual turnover in the current and previous calendar year**; insert s.6(1)(e); non-established persons cannot use s.6(1)(b)/(c)/(d) except via s.92D |
| 6 | s.59(4)(a) — drop “or in accordance with section 5(2)” |
| 7 | New s.60(4): no deductibility on expenditure for Chapter 5 Part 10 (SME scheme) supplies |
| 8 | s.80 cash-accounting: 90% of **annual turnover** to unregistered persons, or annual turnover not over €2,000,000 |
| 9 | Inserts VATCA Chapter 5 / ss.**92B–92D** (cross-border SME scheme) |
| 10 | Inserts Sch.9 Part 3 |

## 92B definitions

- annual turnover = consideration excl. tax from taxable supplies + listed exempt supplies, excluding capital-asset disposals
- Union threshold = **€100,000**
- SME exemption scheme number ends with **EX**

## 92C — State is Member State of establishment

Irish-established person may use another Member State’s SME scheme if Union turnover (this year and last) ≤ €100,000, host-state threshold is met both years, and no EX number has been issued elsewhere. Prior electronic notification to Revenue; Revenue keeps the SME register and issues the EX number within **35 working days**. Quarterly electronic report due within one month after each calendar quarter.

Do not treat 2010-enacted VATCA as if this Chapter did not exist.
