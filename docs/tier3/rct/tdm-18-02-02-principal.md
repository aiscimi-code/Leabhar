# TDM 18-02-02 — Who is a Principal Contractor (Feb 2026)

Read with TCA s.530 / s.530A. FA 2025 s.21 amended housing-legislation references in s.530A; it did not change the range of principals.

A principal to whom RCT applies is:

(a) A person who, for the whole or part of the relevant operations, is the contractor under another relevant contract (a subbie who themselves subcontracts).

(b) A person carrying on a business which includes:
- erection of buildings or development of land (s.639(1)) or manufacture/treatment/extraction of construction materials (concrete, tarmac, sandpits, site clearance); or
- meat processing in an establishment approved under the Fresh Meat / Fresh Poultry-meat Regulations; or
- forestry operations.

s.530A(2): a builder or land developer is **not** a principal only because they erect or develop a building for their own use/occupation or that of employees. Same exclusion for short-term letting of 35 years or less.

(c) A person **connected** with a company carrying on construction, land development, meat processing or forestry must operate RCT on payments to a subcontractor under a relevant contract.

RCT deduction rates (subcontractor compliance): **0% / 20% / 35%**. Not a VAT rate.
