# TDM 18-02-05 — RCT for subcontractors

A subcontractor is the contractor under a relevant contract with a s.530A principal, in construction, forestry or meat processing — not an employee.

Rates: **0%** (up to date), **20%** (substantially up to date), **35%** (poor record or unregistered). New eReg subcontractors start at 20% and can request a ROS rate review.

The principal notifies the contract and each payment. After notification Revenue issues a deduction authorisation; the subcontractor should get a copy if tax is withheld. Credit for RCT deducted is claimed on the income tax / CT return.
