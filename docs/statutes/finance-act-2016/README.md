s.46 substitutes the dual-use calculation in VATCA s.61, the words quoted by `vat.dual_use_apportionment`. The LRC dates that substitution 25 December 2016. s.47(1)(b)(i) substitutes s.86(1), the words quoted by `vat.flat_rate_farmer_purchase`, and states the addition as 5.4 per cent. The LRC dates that substitution 1 January 2017.
