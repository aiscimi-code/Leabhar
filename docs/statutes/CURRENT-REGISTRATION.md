# VAT registration — current

Official: https://www.revenue.ie/en/vat/vat-registration/who-should-register-for-vat/vat-thresholds.aspx

Thresholds apply to **calendar-year** taxable turnover (VAT-exclusive). Below threshold: may elect. Above: must register.

| Case | Threshold |
|---|---:|
| Services only | **€42,500** |
| Goods made from zero-rated materials, charged at reduced or standard | **€42,500** |
| Goods only (other than the row above) | **€85,000** |
| Mixed goods + services where ≥90% of turnover is goods (other than the manufactured-from-zero row) | **€85,000** |
| Intra-Community distance sales of goods + cross-border TBE into the State (one establishment only) | **€10,000** |
| Intra-Community acquisitions | **€41,000** |

No threshold:

- received services (self-account) — https://www.revenue.ie/en/vat/vat-on-services/exceptions-general-place-supply-rules-services/received-services/index.aspx
- non-established person supplying taxable goods or services to taxable customers in the State, unless the VAT SME scheme applies (SI 69/2025)

Domestic SME exemption uses the same €42,500 / €85,000 figures. Eligibility is tested on **both** the current and previous calendar year (Revenue SME TDM).

Superseded — do not cite: €75,000 goods / €37,500 services (pre-1 Jan 2025).
