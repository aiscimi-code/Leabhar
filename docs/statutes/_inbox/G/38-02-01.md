2.     Help about Corporation Tax
The Revenue website has information on Corporation Tax and about iXBRL.

Mandatory Electronic Filing of payments and returns using Revenue's Online Service
(ROS), is part of Revenue's strategy to establish the use of electronic channels as the
normal way of conducting tax business. Companies are among the categories of
taxpayers required to pay and file electronically.

The mandatory eFiling obligation extends to the submission of certain financial
statements in iXBRL.
