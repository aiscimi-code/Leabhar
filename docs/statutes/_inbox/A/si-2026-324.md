# S.I. No. 324 of 2026

Notice of the making of this Statutory Instrument was published in

“Iris Oifigiúil” of
14
th July,
2026.

I, SIMON HARRIS, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by

section 45

(3) of the

Finance Act 2025

(No. 18 of 2025), hereby order as follows:

1. This Order may be cited as the Finance Act 2025 (Section 45) (Commencement) Order 2026.

2. The 10th day of July 2026 is appointed as the day on which

section 45

of the

Finance Act 2025

(No. 18 of 2025) shall come into operation.

GIVEN under my Official Seal,

7 July, 2026.

SIMON HARRIS,

Minister for Finance.
