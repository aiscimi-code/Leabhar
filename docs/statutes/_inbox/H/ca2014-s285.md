# Companies Act 2014 s.285 (LRC revised)

285

Retention of accounting records

285.
An accounting record required to be kept by
section 281
or information or a return referred to in
section 283
(2)
shall be preserved by the company concerned for a period of at least 6 years after
the end of the financial year containing the latest date to which the record, information
or return relates.
