# Companies Act 2014 s.280E (LRC revised)

280E

Micro companies regime

280E.
Where a company qualifies as a micro company in accordance with
section 280D
, then, as provided in this Part, different rules may be applied (in this Act referred
to as the "micro companies regime") to the company in respect of financial statements
and reports for a financial year in relation to which that company so qualifies as
a micro company.
