# European Union (Value-Added Tax) Regulations 2025 (S.I. No. 69 of 2025)

I, PASCHAL DONOHOE, Minister for Finance, in exercise of the powers conferred on me by section 3 of the European Communities Act 1972 (No. 27 of 1972) and for the purpose of giving further effect to Council Directive 2006/112/EC as amended by Council Directive (EU) 2020/285, hereby make the following regulations:

1. These Regulations may be cited as the European Union (Value-Added Tax) Regulations 2025.

2. In these Regulations, “Act of 2010” means the Value-Added Tax Consolidation Act 2010 (No. 31 of 2010).

3. Section 2 of the Act of 2010 is amended—
(a) in subsection (1), by the insertion of the following definition: “ ‘annual turnover’ has the meaning assigned to it by section 92B;”, and
(b) in subsection (4A) as set out in the official instrument.

4. Section 5 of the Act of 2010 is amended by the deletion of subsection (2).

5. Section 6 of the Act of 2010 is amended so that registration thresholds are measured by annual turnover (€85,000 goods / €42,500 services), with special rules for persons not established in the State.

6. Section 59(4)(a) of the Act of 2010 is amended by the deletion of “or in accordance with section 5(2)” after “by election”.

7. Section 60 of the Act of 2010 is amended by insertion of subsection (4): a person shall not deduct any tax on expenditure incurred for the purpose of supplies made in accordance with Chapter 5 of Part 10.

8. Section 80(1) is amended so cash-receipts basis eligibility uses annual turnover, including a €2,000,000 12-month cap.

9. Part 10 of the Act of 2010 is amended by inserting Chapter 5 after section 92A — Cross-border special scheme for small enterprises:

- **s.92B** Definitions: annual turnover; Member State annual turnover threshold; Member State of establishment / exemption; SME exemption scheme; SME exemption scheme number (suffix EX); Union annual turnover; Union threshold **€100,000**.
- **s.92C** Scheme where the State is the Member State of establishment: prior electronic notification, SME register, EX number, 35-working-day grant, quarterly turnover reports, 15-day notice if Union threshold exceeded, cancellation of EX number.
- **s.92D** Scheme where the State is the Member State of exemption for a person established elsewhere in the Community.

10. Schedule 9 of the Act of 2010 is amended by inserting Part 3 (sections 92B, 92C, 92D).

GIVEN under my Official Seal, 6 March 2025. PASCHAL DONOHOE, Minister for Finance.
