# VATCA 2010 s.46 — Rates of tax

Section 46 charges tax at the rates specified in the Act as amended. Schedules 2 and 3 list zero-rated and reduced-rate supplies. The standard rate and the second reduced rate are substituted by successive Finance Acts.

Finance Act 2024 s.79 amended s.46(1):

- paragraph (ca): insert reference to paragraph 12A (low emissions heat pumps) from 1 January 2025;
- paragraph (caa): substitute “30 April 2025” for “31 October 2024” from 2 October 2024.
