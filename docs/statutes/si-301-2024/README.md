# si-301-2024

European Union (Adjustments of Size Criteria for Certain Companies and
Groups) Regulations 2024 (S.I. No. 301 of 2024), as made; in operation on
1 July 2024 (reg. 2).

- Regs 4–8 substitute the turnover and balance sheet figures in CA 2014
  ss.280A(3), 280B(4), 280D(3)(b), 280F(3) and 280G(4). Each states the
  figure it replaces, which is where the earlier figures (€12m / €6m small,
  €700,000 / €350,000 micro, €40m / €20m medium) are quoted from.
- Reg. 9 inserts s.280I: the company elects whether the new figures apply to
  each financial year beginning on or after 1 January 2024, or on or after
  1 January 2023. Employee thresholds are not amended.
