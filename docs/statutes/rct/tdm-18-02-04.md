# TDM 18-02-04 — RCT for principals

FA 2011 s.20 put eRCT in TCA ss.530A–530V. Since 1 Jan 2012 principals deal with Revenue only through ROS.

1. **Contract notification** — when the relevant contract is entered; includes a declaration it is not employment; principal must verify the subcontractor’s identity first.
2. **Payment notification** — immediately before each payment; gross amount is VAT-exclusive if reverse charge applies. Contract notification must already exist.
3. **Deduction authorisation** — Revenue replies with 0 / 20 / 35%. Copy goes to the subcontractor if tax is withheld. Paying off-authorisation attracts a penalty.
4. **Deduction summary** — at period end; becomes the return if unamended.
5. **Pay the RCT** by the due date.

A new principal must notify Revenue within **21 days** of the first relevant contract (eReg, myAccount, TR1/TR2).

Who is a principal: s.530A + TDM 18-02-02. Official PDF (18 pages): https://www.revenue.ie/en/tax-professionals/tdm/income-tax-capital-gains-tax-corporation-tax/part-18/18-02-04.pdf
