# TDM 18-02-01 — Relevant Operations

RCT applies to relevant contracts for operations in:
- construction
- forestry
- meat processing

Updated 2025/26 on mixed contracts (construction + sale of land) and temporary installations on site (eBriefs 126/25 and 032/26).

A relevant contract is not a contract of employment. The principal must notify the contract and each payment through eRCT (SI 651/2011). Revenue issues a deduction authorisation before payment.
