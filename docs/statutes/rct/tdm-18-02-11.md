# TDM 18-02-11 — eRCT (full 6 pages, March 2026)

eRCT has run since 2012 under SI 651/2011.

- Principals/subcontractors (and agents) can look up 12 months of payments in ROS and download CSV. Cancelled payments are omitted.
- Re-open a closed contract: Manage RCT → Search Existing Contracts → Edit → overwrite End Date of Work with a future date → Sign and Submit.
- Unreported payment on a closed contract: allowed up to **18 months** after the end date; after that, re-open first.
- Ordinary payment / post-payment on a closed contract: allowed up to **9 months** after the end date; after that, re-open first.
- Closed 9–18 months: only an Unreported Payment Notification can be entered unless the contract is re-opened.
- New subcontractors on eReg start at **20%**. They can Look-Up/Review Deduction Rate in ROS.
- Bulk Rate Review: monthly, live RCT registrations only, by location and corporate/non-corporate. New rate is notified after bulk review or ROS self-review.
