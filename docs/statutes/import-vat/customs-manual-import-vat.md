# Customs Manual on Import VAT (May 2026)

Guidance only. Binding charge is VATCA s.3(b) and postponed accounting is s.53A + SI 734/2020 reg.14A.

## When import VAT arises

VAT is payable on goods imported into the State from outside the EU. It does **not** apply to services.

Union goods from another Member State are not import VAT (intra-Community acquisition instead), unless they are not in free circulation when they enter the State.

Goods held under a duty-suspension arrangement become subject to import VAT when removed into free circulation.

## Customs-parallel rule

VATCA applies Customs Act mechanisms to import VAT as if it were a customs duty. Import VAT generally follows customs treatment for: temporary importation, external transit, temporary storage, customs warehousing, inward processing, drilling-platform admissions, outward processing.

Suspension, reduction, or remission of **customs duty** does **not** by itself relieve import VAT (e.g. many end-use authorisations).

## Postponed accounting

Accountable persons with a live C&E registration may account for import VAT on the VAT3 under s.53A if they meet SI 734/2020 reg.14A conditions (compliance, s.84 records, no conviction under the tax/customs acts, capacity).
