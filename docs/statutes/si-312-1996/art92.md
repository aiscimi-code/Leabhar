# S.I. 312/1996 art. 92 (LRC revised)

Prescribed level of income for excepted self-employed contributor.

92.

The prescribed amount for the purposes of paragraph 3 of Part III of the First Schedule to the Principal Act shall be €5,000 in a contribution year.

Substituted (1.01.2011) by Social Welfare (Consolidated Contributions and Insurability) (Amendment) Regulations 2010 (S.I. No. 684 of 2010), art. 6.
