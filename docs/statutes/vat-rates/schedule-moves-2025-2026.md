# Category moves (headline rates unchanged, mapping changed)

The Revenue 5-column table is not enough after 8 Oct 2025 / 1 Jul 2026.

| Supply | Until | From |
|---|---|---|
| Restaurant, catering, hot takeaway food (not alcohol, bottled water, soft/sports drinks, vegetable juices) | 13.5% to 30 Jun 2026 | **9% from 1 Jul 2026** |
| Hairdressing | 13.5% to 30 Jun 2026 | **9% from 1 Jul 2026** |
| Hotel / guest accommodation | 13.5% | 13.5% (unchanged) |
| Hotel room hire for meetings/events | reduced | **23% from 1 Jan 2026** |
| Qualifying apartments — supply | 13.5% | **9% 8 Oct 2025 – 25 Nov 2025** |
| Qualifying apartments / blocks — supply and construction | 13.5% | **9% 26 Nov 2025 – 31 Dec 2030** |
| Gas and electricity (domestic) | 9% | 9% until **31 Dec 2030** |
| Farmer flat-rate addition | 5.1% in 2025 | **4.5% from 1 Jan 2026** |
| Broiler stock-minding by flat-rate farmer | addition allowed | **excluded from 1 Sep 2025** |

Statute: FA 2025 ss.70–71 (apartments; food/hairdressing), FA 2025 s.68 (thresholds), SI 69/2025 (SME), SI 327/2025 (flat-rate restriction).
Guidance: Revenue TDM restaurant/catering (15 Jun 2026); eBrief 250/25.
