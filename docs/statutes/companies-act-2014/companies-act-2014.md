# Companies Act 2014 — filing basics

LRC revised Act: https://revisedacts.lawreform.ie/eli/2014/act/38/revised/en/html

## s.282 — adequate accounting records

Records must correctly record and explain transactions; enable assets, liabilities, financial position and profit or loss to be determined with reasonable accuracy; enable s.290/293 financial statements and the s.325 directors’ report; and be auditable. Day-to-day entries of money received and expended. Continuous and consistent. Source: https://revisedacts.lawreform.ie/eli/2014/act/38/section/282/revised/en/html

This is separate from VATCA s.84 (VAT records). A company needs both.

## s.280A — small company

Qualifies if it meets **two or more** of: turnover ≤ **€15 million**; balance sheet total ≤ **€7.5 million**; average employees ≤ **50**. Not a holding company or an ineligible company. Source: https://revisedacts.lawreform.ie/eli/2014/act/38/section/280A/revised/en/html

## s.280D / 280E — micro company regime

Smaller thresholds; may use FRS 105.

## s.352 — abridged filing

Small or micro companies that have not elected to prepare group statements may annex abridged financial statements (s.353) plus a special auditors’ report (s.356) instead of full statements. Source: https://revisedacts.lawreform.ie/eli/2014/act/38/section/352/revised/en/html

## ss.358–360 — audit exemption

Available where the small-company conditions are met and no s.334 member notice or ineligible-category block applies.
