# S.I. 651/2011 — eRCT Regulations

Official print: https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/eli/2011/si/651/made/en/print

| Part | Regs | What |
|---|---|---|
| 1 General | 1–2 | Citation, interpretation |
| 2 Registration | 3 | Register of principals |
| 3 Notifications | 4–5 | Contract notification and declaration; payment notification |
| 4 Authorisations | 6+ | Deduction authorisations and deduction summaries |

These regulations put eRCT on ROS: the principal must notify the relevant contract and each relevant payment; Revenue issues a deduction authorisation (0/20/35%) before payment. Admin walkthrough: TDM Part 18-02-11 (March 2026).
