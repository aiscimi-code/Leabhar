# Income Tax and Corporation Tax (Relevant Contracts Tax) Regulations 2011 (S.I. 651/2011)

**SUPERSEDED — do not treat as current regulations.** Per
[`docs/statutes/rct/tdm-18-02-04.md`](../rct/tdm-18-02-04.md) §14 (RCT
Regulations, itself verbatim Revenue guidance): S.I. 651/2011 "were
subsequently revoked and replaced by Income Tax and Corporation Tax
(Relevant Contracts Tax) Regulations 2012 (S.I. No. 576 of 2012)... These
regulations came into effect on 24 December 2012." Regulation 5 of the 2012
Regulations was further amended by S.I. 412/2013, and Regulation 9 by S.I.
5/2015. None of S.I. 576/2012, 412/2013 or 5/2015 are ingested here. This
was discovered while curating RCT rules (docs/RULES_KB.md "RCT") and is
recorded rather than silently worked around — the eRCT deduction-
authorisation regulatory text this repo actually needs is one of those
three later instruments, not this file.

The eRCT regulations (as they stood in 2011, before the 2012 replacement)
— puts the RCT deduction-authorisation workflow on ROS. Read alongside
[`docs/statutes/rct/`](../rct/) (TCA ss.530/530A, the RCT TDMs) — this SI is
the mechanics of *how* RCT is administered, not the charge itself. Kept as
a reference extract only; **not used to back any curated rule** (see
`src/domain/rules/rctCuration.ts`'s own header for why).
