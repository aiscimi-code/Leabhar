# SCHEDULE 9 — Non-application of section 2(4A) to certain provisions

Inserted 31.12.2020 at 11 p.m. by Withdrawal of the United Kingdom from the European Union (Consequential Provisions) Act 2020 s.67.

## Part 1
Section 33, 34, 35, 56(1), 83, 88(8), 91.

## Part 2
Section 35A, 59, 91A, 91D, 91E, 101, 102(3), paragraph 5(1A) of Schedule 2, paragraph 6(1)(c) of Schedule 2.

## Part 3
Section 92B, 92C, 92D.

Inserted (6.03.2025) by European Union (Value-Added Tax) Regulations 2025 (S.I. No. 69 of 2025), reg. 10.
