# VATCA 2010 Schedule 3 — reduced rates (s.46)

Full revised text: https://revisedacts.lawreform.ie/eli/2010/act/31/schedule/3/revised/en/html

Sch.3 lists supplies at a reduced rate. Which reduced rate (13.5% vs 9%) is s.46 as amended by FA 2025.

| Family | Rate now |
|---|---|
| Restaurant, catering, hot takeaway food (not alcohol/soft drinks) | **9% from 1 Jul 2026** |
| Hairdressing | **9% from 1 Jul 2026** |
| Hotel / guest accommodation | **13.5%** |
| Hotel room hire for meetings/events | **23% from 1 Jan 2026** |
| House construction / general building repair | **13.5%** |
| Qualifying apartments — supply and construction | **9% 26 Nov 2025 – 31 Dec 2030** |
| Gas and electricity (domestic) | **9% until 31 Dec 2030** |
| Periodicals, sporting facilities, heat-pump supply-and-install | **9%** |

Alcohol, bottled water, soft drinks, sports drinks and vegetable juices stay 23% even with a meal.

FA 2025 inserted apartment paragraphs 9A/9B. Resolve paragraph numbers on the LRC page, not a 2010 print.
