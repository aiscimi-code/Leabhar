such specifications as are required by regulations.

(2A)(
a
) An

Chapter, and for the purposes of

controls to each such invoice or other document to ensure

the origin of that invoice or other document,

(ii) the integrity of the
content of that invoice or other document, and

(iii) that there is a
reliable audit trail for that invoice or other document and the supply of goods or
services as described therein.

(
b
) The accountable
person shall furnish evidence of the business controls used to comply with

paragraph (a)
as may be required by the Revenue Commissioners and such
evidence shall be subject to such conditions as may be specified in regulations
(if any).
