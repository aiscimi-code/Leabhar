# VATCA 2010 s.75 (revised)

75

Tax due on
intra-Community acquisitions.

[VATA s. 19(1A)]

75
.— Tax chargeable under
section 3
(d)
or
(e)

shall be due—

(
a
) on the 15th day of the month following that
during which the intra-Community acquisition occurs,

(
b
) in case an invoice is issued before the date
specified in
paragraph (a)
by the supplier in another
Member State to the person acquiring the goods, when that
invoice is issued.
