# VATCA 2010 Schedule 2 — zero rate (s.46)

Full revised text: https://revisedacts.lawreform.ie/eli/2010/act/31/schedule/2/revised/en/html

Use this schedule to decide whether a supply is zero-rated.

## Part 1 — international

1. Intra-Community supply of goods dispatched from the State to a person VAT-registered in another Member State, if the supplier has complied with s.82 (or justified the failure).
2. New means of transport dispatched to another Member State.
3. Excisable products moved under the FA 2001 duty-suspension arrangements.
4. Intra-Community transport of goods to/from the Azores or Madeira.
5. Goods a taxable person is deemed to supply under s.91G.

Also zero-rated: imports already consigned to another Member State; IOSS-declared importations; exports from the State out of the Community; work on movable goods then exported; qualifying sea-going vessels and international-route aircraft (supply, repair, hire, fuel/provisioning, direct-needs services); supplies treated as exports to EU institutions and listed international bodies; free-port / customs-free airport supplies between registered persons; traveller’s qualifying goods if s.58(2) is met.

## Part 2 — domestic

Food of a kind used for human consumption that is **not** a restaurant/catering/hot-takeaway supply; books; children’s clothing and footwear; oral medicines; seeds, plants and fertiliser as listed; solar panels supplied and installed on private dwellings and schools.

Restaurant / catering / hot takeaway food leaves Sch.2 and is Sch.3 (9% from 1 July 2026).
