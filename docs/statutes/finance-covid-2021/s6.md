# Finance (Covid-19 and Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2021 s.6

Amendment of section 46 of Act of 2010

6. Section 46 of the Act of 2010 is amended in subsection (1)(cb) by the substitution of “31 August 2022” for “31 December 2021”.
