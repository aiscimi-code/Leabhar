# TCA 1997 s.530 — interpretation of RCT

**Relevant contract** (not employment): the contractor is liable to the principal to carry out relevant operations, to be answerable for others carrying them out, or to furnish labour for them.

**Relevant operations** = construction, forestry or meat processing.

**Construction operations** include building work; works forming part of the land; installation of heating/lighting/air-con/sanitation/water/fire systems; construction-stage cleaning; site clearance, excavation, scaffolding, landscaping, access; haulage for hire of materials/plant for those operations.

From 15 May 1996 a separate relevant contract is deemed to exist with each member of a gang or group where the principal has no relevant payments card.
