# TCA 1997 s.288 — balancing allowances and charges

If an initial or wear-and-tear allowance has been made, a balancing allowance or charge arises when: the plant ceases to belong to the trader; it permanently ceases to be used in the trade; the trade is permanently discontinued; or, for software, a capital grant of a right to use is given.

Balancing allowance: expenditure still unallowed exceeds sale/insurance/salvage/compensation moneys (or there are none).
Balancing charge: those moneys exceed expenditure still unallowed; capped at the aggregate of initial, wear-and-tear, scientific-research and prior balancing allowances.
