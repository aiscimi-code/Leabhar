# TCA 1997 s.18 — Schedule D

Tax under Schedule D is charged on annual profits or gains described in the Cases.

**Case I** — profits or gains from any trade.
**Case II** — profits or gains from any profession.
**Case III** — untaxed interest and other annual profits not charged under other Cases/Schedules.
**Case IV** — residual annual profits or gains.
**Case V** — rents from Irish land/buildings.
