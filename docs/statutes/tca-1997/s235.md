# TCA 1997 s.235

Exemption from income tax or corporation tax for an approved body established solely to promote athletic or amateur games or sports, to the extent the income is applied to that purpose. Revenue may withdraw approval by notice if the body was not established for that sole purpose or exists to secure a tax advantage.
