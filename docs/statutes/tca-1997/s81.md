# TCA 1997 s.81 — General rule as to deductions

The core Case I/II deductibility rule: no sum is deductible unless it is money wholly and exclusively laid out or expended for the purposes of the trade or profession, subject to the specific disallowances in the section (capital withdrawn, capital employed, improvements, notional rent, etc.).
