# TCA 1997 ss.885–887

Official as-enacted text. Later Finance Act amendments are not consolidated here.

## s.886 Obligation to keep certain records

Every person chargeable under Schedule D or F, or to CGT, shall keep records that enable true returns to be made. Records include sums received and expended, sales and purchases, assets and liabilities, and CGT acquisitions/disposals. Retain for 6 years after the transactions (longer if no return was delivered). Electronic storage is subject to s.887.

## s.887 Use of electronic data processing

Records required by the Acts may be stored, maintained, transmitted, reproduced or communicated by any electronic, photographic or other process approved by the Revenue Commissioners, subject to their conditions.

Full section: https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/eli/1997/act/39/section/887/enacted/en/html

## s.885 Obligation to show tax reference number on receipts

Specified persons must state a tax reference number (including VAT registration number) on invoices, credit notes, receipts etc. of £5 or more.
