# Finance Act 2020 (No. 26 of 2020) s.39

Captured for issue #688. The revised VATCA s.46 no longer holds the 2020–2023 wording of paragraph (cb). This section is the insertion, not the 31 August 2023 text.

- Official HTML: https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/eli/2020/act/26/section/39/enacted/en/html
- `s39.html` SHA-256: `c4169141093738acd5b278ba54a78d39904d21b25a2b18c960b049077c018c1e`

Operative text: with effect from 1 November 2020, s.46(1)(c) is amended to refer to paragraphs (ca) and (cb), and (cb) is inserted for the period 1 November 2020 to 31 December 2021, at 9 per cent, for Schedule 3 paragraphs 3(1), 3(3), 7(b) to (e), 8, 11 and 13(3).

Later substitutions of the end date are in `../finance-covid-2021/s6.md`, `../finance-covid-2022/s7.md` and `../finance-act-2023/s5.md`. The 1 July 2026 substitution is already in `../finance-act-2025/`.
