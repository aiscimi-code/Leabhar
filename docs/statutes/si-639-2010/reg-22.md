# S.I. 639/2010 reg.22 — flat-rate farmer invoices

A flat-rate farmer invoice under VATCA s.68(1) must be acknowledged by the farmer and show: date; farmer name and address; customer name, address and registration number (plus EU VAT number if the customer is registered in another Member State); quantity and nature; supply date; consideration exclusive of the addition; rate and amount of the flat-rate addition.

Increase-in-consideration invoices, farmer credit/debit notes and settlement vouchers have parallel particular-lists (s.68(2)–(4)).

Paper invoices must be kept as an exact copy. Electronic farmer invoices follow reg.21.

Live addition rate is **4.5% from 1 Jan 2026**, not the 2010 figure. Broiler stock-minding is excluded from the addition from 1 Sep 2025.
