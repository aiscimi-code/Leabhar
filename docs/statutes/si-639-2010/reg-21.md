# S.I. 639/2010 reg.21 — electronic invoicing

A message issued or received electronically is a valid s.66(2) invoice if it is sent between trading partners by EDI or by advanced electronic signature, and the system can: produce/retain/store the records Revenue may require; print them; allocate a unique identification number; retrieve by partner, date or number; prevent repeat transmission and omitted messages; verify origin/receipt; and keep integrity for the s.84 retention period.

Other electronic means are allowed if the person is satisfied the s.66(2) conditions are met, the same system rules apply, and Revenue is notified.

The person must store the messages plus encryption/signature-creation/verification details, allow Revenue inspection, and print on request.
