# Value-Added Tax Regulations 2010 (S.I. No. 639 of 2010)

Come into operation 1 January 2011. Made under VATCA 2010 s.120.

Full instrument: https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/eli/2010/si/639/made/en/print

## Regulation 20 — Invoices and other documents

The following particulars are specified for purposes of section 66(1) of the Act and are required to be included in every invoice issued, or deemed to be issued, by an accountable person:

(a) the date of issue of the invoice,

(b) a sequential number, based on one or more series, which uniquely identifies the invoice,

(c) the full name, address and registration number of the person who supplied the goods or services,

plus the further particulars in the official regulation (customer details, description, unit price exclusive of VAT, VAT rate breakdown, VAT payable, reverse-charge / intra-Community notations).

Related: reg. 21 electronic invoicing; reg. 22 flat-rate farmers; reg. 23 time limits for invoices and credit notes.

## Regulation 27 — Accounts

(1) The full and true records of all transactions that affect or may affect the accountable person’s liability to tax and entitlement to deductibility, which every accountable person is required to keep in accordance with Chapter 7 of Part 9 and section 124(7) of the Act, shall be entered up to date and include—

(a) in relation to consideration receivable from registered persons — the amounts for every transaction for which an invoice or other document is required to be issued, with a cross-reference to that invoice or document;

(b) in relation to consideration receivable from persons not registered for VAT — a daily total of the consideration receivable, cross-referenced to cash sheets and other relevant records.
