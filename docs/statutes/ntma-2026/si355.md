# S.I. No. 355 of 2026

National Treasury Management Agency (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2026 (Commencement) Order 2026

3. The 22nd day of July 2026 is appointed as the day on which Parts 1 (other than sections 4 and 5), 2, 3 and 4 of the Act of 2026 shall come into operation.

4. The 1st day of August 2026 is appointed as the day on which sections 4 and 5 of, and Schedules 1, 2 and 3 to, the Act of 2026 shall come into operation.

Given under the Official Seal of the Minister for Finance, 21 July 2026.
