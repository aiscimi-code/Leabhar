# National Treasury Management Agency (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 2026 (No. 22 of 2026)

Captured for issue #689. Removes the NAMA vesting-order provisions from VATCA ss.16, 59, 64 and 94.

- Enacted 15 July 2026.
- s.1(2) leaves commencement to ministerial order. The date is not in the Act.
- S.I. No. 355 of 2026, art. 4, appoints 1 August 2026 for sections 4 and 5 and Schedules 1 to 3.
- Official print (includes the Schedules): https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/eli/2026/act/22/enacted/en/print
- Commencement order: https://www.irishstatutebook.ie/eli/2026/si/355/made/en/html
